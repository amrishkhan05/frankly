# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **Be kind** and respectful.
- **Use inclusive language**.
- **Avoid harassment** and discrimination.
- **Report** any violations to the project maintainers.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
